How your data is protected

  • Everyone signs in with their own claude.ai account. The app never sees or stores passwords.
  • Permissions are checked on the server, not just hidden in the app. Only mentors and leads with Editor access can post meetings, announcements, news and CAD.
  • Roles can only be changed by mentors. A squirrel can't promote themselves.
  • Team data is hidden from anyone outside the team, including people with a public link.
  • Your read receipts are private to you. Your RSVP and high score are visible to the team.
  • Acorn only sees the schedule, announcements and handbook, and runs on your own account.
